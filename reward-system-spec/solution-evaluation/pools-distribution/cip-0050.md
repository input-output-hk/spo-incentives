# CIP-0050 — Pledge Leverage-Based Staking Rewards

> **Status:** Active 2026/04/22. Stake-cap-layer candidate. Evaluation against V2 §2 grid. Sources in §7.

## Executive summary

- **Verdict.** Sharp §3.2 instrument — makes pledge a binding constraint on reward-eligible stake. Single scalar parameter $L$; smallest governance surface in the bundle.
- **Hard break.** Zero pledge → zero rewards. Pool-splitting becomes revenue-neutral (summed cap = single-pool cap). Honest small pools unaffected at $L = 100$.
- **Sequencing constraint.** Does *not* guarantee §3.1. Must be deployed **after** a fee-layer instrument (CIP-0082 staged path) to avoid pushing the sub-threshold tail into the structural floor.
- **Entity gap.** CEX / IVaaS custodial operators with native ADA capacity can meet any reasonable $L$ — concentration channel not closed at entity level.

## 1. Mechanism

**Formula.** Modifies reward-eligible stake used in the RSS third equation:

$$\sigma'^{(50)}_{i} = \min\left(\sigma_{i},\ 1/k,\ L\cdot p_{i}\right)$$

- $\sigma_i$ = pool total stake (pledge + delegated). $p_i$ = pledge. $k$ = target-pool count. $L$ = new leverage cap.
- Stake above this cap yields no additional reward.

**Design surface.**

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| New parameter | $L$ (`pledgeLeverage`) — dimensionless |
| Range | $1 \leq L \leq 10{,}000$ |
| Sweet spot (per CIP simulation) | $L \in [10, 100]$ |
| Layer | Stake-cap (applied *before* reward curve) |
| Fee-layer split | Untouched (`minPoolCost`, `minPoolMargin` unchanged) |
| Governance knobs | 1 |

**Intent.** Make the zero-pledge strategy economically unviable. Current $a_0 = 0.3$ only produces ~22 % yield gap between zero-pledge and fully-pledged pools — "statistically unnoticed" by delegators and dominated by epoch-to-epoch variance. $L$ converts pledge from nudge to binding constraint.

### 1.1 Structural properties

| Property | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Zero pledge | **Hard break** — $L \cdot 0 = 0$, eligible stake collapses to zero regardless of delegation |
| Pool-splitting | **Revenue-neutral** — pledge $P$ split across $N$ pools summed cap = $L \cdot P$ = single-pool cap |
| Honest small pools | **Unaffected at $L = 100$, $k = 500$.** A 500 k ADA pool needs only 5 k pledge (1 %) to escape the cap |
| Full-saturation requirement | ~1 % pledge ratio at $L = 100$; ~750 k ADA pledge at full saturation |

### 1.2 Anchoring data from the CIP text (epoch 560)

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Zero-pledge pools | 473 |
| Stake held by zero-pledge pools | 2.74 B ADA |
| MAV (economic cost of 50 %-stake Sybil attack via low-pledge) | ~53.87 M ADA |
| Nakamoto coefficient at $L = 100$ (simulation) | ~160 |
| `k`-only pathology at low $a_0$ | Nakamoto drops 142 → 116 when `k` sweeps 250 → 2 000 |

## 2. Milestone coverage

| V2 milestone | Verdict | Where it bites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §3.1 Operator viability | Neutral to mildly negative | Low-pledge pools lose eligible stake; pressure on subscale operators without pledge |
| §3.2 Pledge as signal | **Direct, strong** | Pledge becomes a binding constraint on the reward pot; zero-pledge hard break is a sharp economic signal |
| §3.3 Delegator yield | Reshaping, not levelling | Delegators to over-leveraged pools see per-unit yield collapse; viable-pool landscape contracts to pledged pools |
| §3.4 Concentration | **Pool-level strong, entity-level partial** | Pool-splitting revenue-neutral removes the primary MPO incentive; custodial operators with native pledge remain unconstrained |
| §4.1 Pot survival | No effect | Stake-cap layer only |
| §4.2 Fee-gen population | No effect | — |
| §4.3 Price robustness | **Structurally robust** | $L$ is dimensionless; the *ratio* constraint is price-invariant. More price-robust than CIP-0037's absolute-pledge anchors |
| §4.4 Governability | **Strongest in bundle** | A single governable scalar — minimum parameter-surface expansion |

**Dependency-chain note.** Targets §3.2 and §3.4; does *not* guarantee §3.1. Deploying a low-$L$ regime without companion fee-layer reform risks pushing the sub-threshold viable-pool tail further into the structural floor. Any V2-compatible package places CIP-0050 **after** a §3.1/§3.3 fee-layer instrument.

## 3. Validation plan

Gated per [`../methodology.md`](../methodology.md). The spine is G1 + G2 + G3; G4 and G5 are directional; G6 is the production contract; G7 is supplementary.

### 3.1 G1 — V2 spec conformance

Per-milestone structural fit, independent of parameter values:

| V2 milestone | Structural fit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §3.1 Operator viability | **Out of reach** | Formula acts on reward-eligible stake $\sigma'$; fee split ($\texttt{minPoolCost}$, margin) untouched. Viability floor must come from a fee-layer instrument |
| §3.2 Pledge as signal | **Delivered** | $L\cdot p$ term makes pledge a binding cap on rewarded stake — zero-pledge hard break; pledge strictly monotonic on the binding branch |
| §3.3 Delegator yield | **Out of reach** | No fee-layer effect. Delegator yield redistributes only via §3.2 displacement |
| §3.4 Concentration | **Delivered pool-level, gap entity-level** | Pool-splitting revenue-neutral (theorem §3.2 G2); custodial operators with native pledge ≫ $L\cdot p$ threshold unconstrained — structural entity-level gap |
| §4.1 Pot survival | **Out of reach** | Pot composition / reserve draw unchanged |
| §4.2 Fee-gen population | **Out of reach** | Transaction-submitter economics unchanged |
| §4.3 Price robustness | **Delivered** | $L$ dimensionless; the cap is a ratio — price-invariant by construction |
| §4.4 Governability | **Delivered** | Single scalar; smallest governance surface in the bundle |

**Contingency.** §3.2 claim is contingent on §3.1 being delivered first by a fee-layer instrument. Without prior fee-layer relief, §3.2 displaces stake from subthreshold low-pledge pools into the structural floor — a net regression on the population V2 §3.1 aims to protect.

**G1 verdict.** CIP-0050 passes G1 on §3.2, §3.4 (pool-level), §4.3, §4.4. G1 flags structural entity-level gap on §3.4, out-of-reach on §3.1 / §3.3 / §4.1 / §4.2, and a §3.1-contingency on the §3.2 claim.

### 3.2 G2 — Analytical properties

| Property | Claim | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero-pledge hard break | $L\cdot 0 = 0$ ⇒ eligible stake is zero regardless of delegation | Theorem |
| Pool-splitting | Summed cap across $N$ pools sharing pledge $p$ equals $L\cdot p$ = single-pool cap | Theorem (revenue-neutral) |
| Monotonicity in pledge | $\partial \sigma' / \partial p \geq 0$ on the binding branch | Theorem |
| Price invariance | $L$ is dimensionless; the cap is a ratio | Theorem |
| Governance surface | One scalar ($L$) | Direct count |

### 3.3 G3 — Mainnet-snapshot counterfactual

Apply $\sigma'^{(50)} = \min(\sigma, 1/k, L\cdot p)$ at $L = 100$ against the V2 diagnostic anchor epoch. Immediate effects, before any behavioural response:

| Population | Size | Immediate effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero-pledge pools (epoch-560 anchor) | 473 pools, 2.74 B ADA | Reward-eligible stake ↓ to **0** |
| Pools with pledge ratio < 1 % | From diagnostic leverage histogram | Capped at $100\cdot p$ — surplus stake unrewarded |
| Pools with pledge ratio ≥ 1 % | Majority of active set | Unchanged — cap does not bind |
| CEX / IVaaS clusters | 10 entities, 181 pools | Native ADA pledge ≫ 1 %; cap does not bind |
| MAV (CIP-cited, 50 %-stake Sybil) | ≈ 53.87 M ADA | Sybil-cost floor rises by the unpledged-displaced amount |

### 3.5 G5 — Population best-response reasoning

*Assumptions, not predictions.*

| Population | Pre-CIP-0050 strategy | Plausible response under $L = 100$ | Threatens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zero-pledge MPO | Operate $N$ pools with ε pledge | Acquire pledge (best case) / exit / persist with zero rewards | §3.2 if a loophole exists |
| Pledged MPO | Operate $N$ pools sharing fixed pledge | Consolidate to fewer, larger pools | — (intended) |
| CEX / IVaaS | Large native-pledged pools | Unchanged — cap does not bind | §3.4 entity-level gap persists |
| Viable independent single-pool | Pledge sufficient for 1/k saturation | Unchanged | — |
| Sub-threshold independent | Below viability regardless of $L$ | Unchanged by $L$ alone; still exposed to fixed-fee regressivity | §3.1 unresolved without CIP-0082 pairing |
| Delegator | Brand / yield mix | Migrate from capped low-pledge pools to pledged alternatives | — (intended) |

| Stage | Action | What to measure | Rollback trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activation | Introduce $L = 10{,}000$ (near-inactive) at HFC | Baseline MAV, subthreshold count, leverage-ratio histogram | N/A — calibration epoch |

## 4. Transition and governance

| Aspect | Path / Risk |
| --- | --- |
| On-chain path | Parameter introduction + HFC activation (new parameter, not a plain update) |
| Staging (not in CIP) | Natural ramp: introduce at $L = 10 000$ (near-inactive) → step down toward target. Governance choice. |
| Re-registration | Not required; $L$ enters ledger reward calculation only |
| Migration shock | **Without a ramp**, the 473 zero-pledge pools go from near-full to zero rewards in one epoch. Hard shock to those pools and their delegates. |
| Constitutional fit | Compatible with parameter-change chapter; HFC required because it's scope-extension, not scalar update |

## 5. Interaction audit

### 5.1 Same-layer — CIP-0050 ↔ CIP-0037

Technical composition:

$$\sigma'^{(50+37)}_{i} = \min\left(\sigma_{i},\ \sigma^{\text{dyn,sat}}(p_i),\ L\cdot p_{i}\right)$$

**Not canonical without explicit governance adoption.** The two are alternative formulations of the same §3.2/§3.4 intent:

| Style | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| CIP-0050 | Harder floor on pledge-to-stake ratio via blunt inequality |
| CIP-0037 | Smoother gradient via pledge-indexed saturation curve |

A coherent V2 package **picks one stake-cap instrument**. Stacking as belt-and-braces pays in parameter-surface complexity and joint-calibration risk. A head-to-head comparison is maintained as a separate working document.

### 5.2 Cross-layer — CIP-0050 ↔ CIP-0023 / CIP-0082

Composes cleanly with either fee-layer candidate. Layers are sequentially ordered: stake-cap produces $\sigma'^{(50)}$ → reward curve produces $PoolPot^{\text{actual}}_i$ → fee-layer split applies. No precedence rule required.

**End-to-end dependency-chain pairing:** **CIP-0082 (fee layer) + CIP-0050 (stake-cap layer)** — viability-first path in the house recommendation package.

### 5.3 Interaction with `k`

- **Raise `k` alone.** Delegation surface expands faster than Sybil economics tighten. Nakamoto drops 142 → 116 at low $a_0$.
- **Deploy $L$ first, raise `k` second.** MPO fleet expansion constrained; `k` raise translates to *new operators* filling slots. $L$ converts a `k` raise **from concentration risk into decentralisation lever**.

### 5.4 Dependency-chain ordering

1. Fee-layer instrument (CIP-0082 staged path) — secures §3.1 + §3.3.
2. CIP-0050, ramped from $L = 10 000$ toward $L \in [10, 100]$ — activates §3.2 + §3.4 pool-level.
3. `k` calibration (CIP-0082 stages 3–4) — after steps 1–2, leveraging $L$-induced MPO consolidation.

## 6. Gaps against V2

| Gap | Why |
| --- | --- |
| §3.1 guarantee | Pledge-ratio cap does not lift the structural fixed-fee floor; subscale pledged pools still face fee regressivity |
| Custodial constraint | CEX/IVaaS entities with large native ADA holdings meet any reasonable $L$ with pledge to spare — §3.4 entity-level gap |
| §4.1, §4.2 | No effect on pot composition or fee-generating population |

**Role in a V2 package.** Sharpest §3.2 instrument in the bundle. Pairing with CIP-0082 is the **minimum viable V2-aligned package** on the dependency-chain axis that is addressable via parameter-level CIPs.
