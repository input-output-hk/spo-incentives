# CIP-0037 — Dynamic Saturation Based on Pledge

> **Status:** Active 2026/04/22. Stake-cap-layer candidate. Evaluation against V2 §2 grid. Sources in §7.

## Executive summary

- **Verdict.** Smooth §3.2 instrument — saturation is a monotone function of pledge. Softer than CIP-0050 at the extremes, stronger gradient in the mid-pledge range.
- **No hard break.** Zero pledge still retains the 20 %-of-K floor; honest small operators protected by construction.
- **Structural cost.** Three anchor parameters $(e, \ell, p_{100\%})$ expand governance surface more than CIP-0050's single $L$.
- **Price sensitivity.** 500 k ADA anchor is absolute — §4.3 calibration shifts with ADA price.
- **Sequencing constraint.** Like CIP-0050, does *not* guarantee §3.1. Must follow a fee-layer instrument.

## 1. Mechanism

**Formula.** Pledge-indexed saturation:

$$\text{sat}(p) = \text{orig\_sat} \cdot \max\left(e,\ \min\left(\tfrac{1}{k},\ \tfrac{p}{\text{orig\_sat}} \cdot \ell\right)\right)$$

Outer clamp in reference code: `final_sat = max(new_sat, orig_sat)` — status-quo preservation lower bound. Interaction with the inner floor $e$ needs governance clarification at implementation.

**Design surface.**

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| New parameters | $(e,\ \ell,\ p_{100\%})$ — three anchor parameters |
| Reference $e$ (floor multiplier) | 0.2 → 20 % of K minimum |
| Reference $\ell$ (leverage constant) | 125 |
| Reference $p_{100\%}$ (anchor pledge) | 500 k ADA → 100 % of K saturation |
| `orig_sat` | Supply / $k$ ≈ 67.44 M ADA at $k = 500$ |
| Layer | Stake-cap (applied *before* reward curve) |
| Fee-layer split | Untouched (`minPoolCost`, `minPoolMargin` unchanged) |
| Governance knobs | 3 (vs CIP-0050's 1) |

**Intent.** The CIP targets the multi-pool pledge dilution pathology:

> *"An SPO might have a pledge of 30,000 ADA across 10 pools, while another SPO might have 1,000,000 pledge but only has 1 pool with a small amount of active stake. Since there is no technical advantage to having a high pledge, the meaning and purpose of a pledge is redundant."*

Under CIP-0037, splitting a fixed pledge budget across more pools moves each pool left on the saturation curve — per-pool capacity drops below the ceiling proportionally.

### 1.1 Worked calibration (reference parameters)

Saturation as a function of pledge at $e = 0.2$, $\ell = 125$, $p_{100\%} = 500 \text{k}$, $k = 500$:

| Pledge (ADA) | Saturation (ADA) | % of K | Regime |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 50 000 | 13 487 713 | 20.0 % | Floor |
| 100 000 | 13 487 713 | 20.0 % | Floor |
| 150 000 | 18 750 000 | 27.8 % | Slope |
| 250 000 | 31 250 000 | 46.3 % | Slope |
| 500 000 | 62 500 000 | 92.7 % | Slope |
| 750 000 | 67 438 565 | 100 % | Ceiling |
| 1 000 000 | 67 438 565 | 100 % | Ceiling |
| 2 000 000 | 67 438 565 | 100 % | Ceiling |

Three regimes: flat floor at 20 % of K up to ~125 k pledge, linear slope from ~150 k to ~670 k pledge, flat ceiling at 100 % of K above ~700 k pledge.

### 1.2 MPO fleet-split penalty (CIP example — 1 M ADA pledge)

| Pools | Pledge / pool | Current scheme | CIP-0037 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 000 000 | 100 % of K | 100 % of K |
| 2 | 500 000 | 100 % of K | 100 % of K |
| 4 | 250 000 | 100 % of K | 50 % of K |
| 8 | 125 000 | 100 % of K | 25 % of K |
| 16 | 62 500 | 100 % of K | 12.5 % of K |

Current: absorbable stake rises linearly with pool count. CIP-0037: plateaus at ~130 M ADA (≈ 2 × `orig_sat`) for this pledge budget.

### 1.3 Structural properties

| Property | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Gradient style | **Smooth** — three-regime monotone function; no binary cap |
| Zero pledge | **Soft floor** — retains 20 % of K saturation (sharp contrast with CIP-0050's zero) |
| Small-pool protection | Floor $e = 0.2$ is deliberate tail-protection — honest modest-pledge operators unharmed |
| MPO splitting | Revenue-decreasing past the anchor — per-pool saturation drops below ceiling |
| Fee-layer | Untouched — operates before the reward curve |

## 2. Milestone coverage

| V2 milestone | Verdict | Where it bites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §3.1 Operator viability | Neutral to mildly negative | Low-pledge pools lose ceiling capacity; subscale operators with zero pledge still retain 20 %-of-K floor but lose ceiling |
| §3.2 Pledge as signal | **Direct, strong** | Saturation is a monotone function of pledge; the 500 k-ADA anchor makes pledge the primary capacity lever in the mid-range |
| §3.3 Delegator yield | Reshaping, not levelling | Delegators redirected toward higher-pledge pools; viable-pool landscape contracts |
| §3.4 Concentration | **Pool-level partial, entity-level indirect** | MPO with fixed pledge gains nothing splitting below the anchor; custodial operators with large native pledge unconstrained |
| §4.1 Pot survival | No effect | Stake-cap layer only |
| §4.2 Fee-gen population | No effect | — |
| §4.3 Price robustness | **Sensitive** | The 500 k-ADA anchor is absolute. At lower ADA price, pledge needed for 100 % saturation becomes a larger fraction of operator capital; at higher prices, smaller operators reach the ceiling |
| §4.4 Governability | Moderate | Three anchor parameters — richer than CIP-0050, coarser than CIP-0082's four stages |

**Dependency-chain note.** Targets §3.2 and §3.4 pool-level; risks worsening §3.1 for subscale tail. Deploying it without first securing §3.1 via a fee-layer instrument risks displacing delegation *away* from the subthreshold population V2 §3.1 aims to protect.

## 3. Validation plan

Analytical properties and mainnet counterfactual arithmetic carry the verdict; historical analogies, best-response reasoning, governance-ramp, and simulation calibrate uncertainty.

### 3.1 Analytical properties (high trust)

Proved directly from the formula $\text{sat}(p) = \text{orig\_sat} \cdot \max(e,\ \min(\tfrac{1}{k},\ \tfrac{p}{\text{orig\_sat}} \cdot \ell))$:

| Property | Statement | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monotonicity in pledge | $\partial\, \text{sat}/\partial p \ge 0$ | Pledge-as-signal (§3.2) holds pool-wise |
| Soft floor at $e$ | $\text{sat}(0) = e \cdot \text{orig\_sat} > 0$ | Zero pledge keeps 20 %-of-K capacity (≠ CIP-0050's hard break) |
| Three-regime shape | Floor / linear slope / ceiling | Binding regime determined by $(p,\ \ell,\ p_{100\%})$ — see §1.1 worked calibration |
| MPO fleet-split penalty | Splitting a fixed pledge budget below the anchor shrinks per-pool ceiling linearly | §1.2 worked calibration: 1 M ADA across 16 pools → 12.5 % of K each |
| Price coupling | 500 k-ADA anchor is absolute, not a ratio | §4.3 calibration depends on ADA price; pairs poorly with monetary stress without re-peg |
| Governance surface | 3 scalar parameters $(e, \ell, p_{100\%})$ | Richer than CIP-0050's single $L$; joint recalibration required on any $k$ change |

The analytical layer suffices to accept §3.2 and the pool-level part of §3.4 as structurally delivered at reference parameters; it suffices to reject any claim of automatic §3.1 repair.

### 3.2 Mainnet snapshot counterfactual (high trust)

Arithmetic on the recent-epoch snapshot, at reference parameters $(e=0.2,\ \ell=125,\ p_{100\%}=500\text{k})$, $k=500$. Populations from the diagnostic census.

| Population | Size | Pre-CIP-0037 ceiling | Post-CIP-0037 ceiling | Immediate effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zero-pledge pools | 473 | `orig_sat` | 20 % · `orig_sat` (floor) | Capacity reduced, not eliminated — contrast with CIP-0050 |
| Low-pledge pools ($p < 100$ k) | large | `orig_sat` | 20 %-of-K floor | Floor binds until pledge reaches ~125 k |
| Mid-pledge pools ($p \in [125\text{k}, 670\text{k}]$) | — | `orig_sat` | Linear slope $\in [\sim 20\%, 100\%]$ of K | Slope regime — the CIP's active operating range |
| High-pledge pools ($p \ge p_{100\%}$) | CEX/IVaaS typical | `orig_sat` | 100 % of K (ceiling) | Unchanged |
| MPO fleet entities | 85 | Per-pool `orig_sat` | Per §1.2 fleet-split table | Fleet-split penalty binds for pledge budgets < 16 × $p_{100\%}$ |
| Viable independent 1-pool operators | 283 | `orig_sat` | Floor or slope, depending on pledge | Protected by the 20 % floor; displacement risk only via yield competition, not capacity |

**Calibration anchor.** The diagnostic's pool leverage-distribution histogram identifies where the active fleet sits relative to the $p_{100\%}$ anchor today; this is the empirical input that determines whether the reference parameters bind earlier, at, or later than the existing effective saturation for MPO entities.

### 3.3 Historical analogies (medium trust)

No direct historical analogue — CIP-0037 introduces a new rewards-calculation rule (§4: hard fork combinator required). Weakly informative references:

| Analogue | Information content |
| --- | --- |
| `k: 150 → 500` (Cardano, Aug 2020) | Parameter-space move succeeded on a single scalar; does not generalise to a 3-parameter curve change |
| Early-era pledge distributions | Calibration-era data for where the slope regime would have bound — suggestive only |

### 3.4 Game-theoretic best responses (assumptions, not predictions)

Per-population reasoning, conditional on delegator behaviour staying within the rational-yield assumption:

| Population | Assumed response | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MPO fleets with fixed pledge budget | Consolidate pools upward toward the anchor; retire marginal pools below the floor regime | Medium — depends on operator cost of running vs closing a pool |
| CEX / IVaaS with native pledge ≥ $p_{100\%}$ | No structural change — ceiling unchanged | High — ceiling is mechanically unchanged |
| Viable independent 1-pool operators with low pledge | Capacity constrained to floor regime; yield pressure if delegators migrate toward higher-pledge pools | Medium — the floor protects capacity, not competitive yield |
| Delegators | Migration toward higher-pledge pools where the slope regime rewards pledge | Low — brand and convenience effects (see CIP-0082 §3.4) can blunt this |

### 3.5 Governance ramp and rollback

Production contract, not a simulation:

| Stage | Value of $e$ | Duration | Rollback trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 1.0 (curve flat at 100 % of K — status quo) | Activation epoch | — |
| 1 | 0.75 | 10–20 epochs | Subthreshold pool count rises monotonically across the stage |
| 2 | 0.50 | 10–20 epochs | MAV trends up, or MPO fleet consolidation yields no entity-level Nakamoto gain |
| 3 | 0.20 (reference) | Indefinite | Independent-SPO count falls past the §3.2 baseline |

**Joint-recalibration requirement.** Any $k$ change after stage 0 requires joint recalibration of $(e, \ell, p_{100\%})$ — these are coupled through `orig_sat = total_supply / k` and the absolute ADA anchor.

### 3.6 Exploratory simulation (low trust, supplementary)

Simulator used as a supplementary tool: the Rewards-Sharing-Simulation-Engine (separate repository), under active improvement 2026. Runs here are diagnostic, not load-bearing.

Ablation questions:

- $e$ sweep $\in \{0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.25\}$ — tail-protection vs Sybil-resistance trade-off magnitude.
- $\ell$ sweep $\in \{75, 100, 125, 150, 200\}$ — slope-steepness sensitivity.
- $p_{100\%}$ sweep $\in \{250\text{k},\ 500\text{k},\ 1\text{M},\ 2\text{M}\}$ — cross-price calibration shape.
- Head-to-head with CIP-0050 under matched §3.2 intent — entity-level MAV delta.
- Delegation redistribution pattern under different brand-pull assumptions — see CIP-0082 §3.6.

None of the above can by itself support or refute a V2 verdict; all must be reported with explicit assumption disclosure.

## 4. Transition and governance

| Aspect | Path / Risk |
| --- | --- |
| On-chain path | **Rewards-calculation code change** — hard fork combinator (HFC) required. Not a pure parameter update |
| Parameter surface | Three anchor parameters $(e, \ell, p_{100\%})$ — complexity cost at governance layer |
| Staging (natural ramp) | Start at $e = 1.0$ (curve flat at 100 % of K — equivalent to status quo), lower $e$ incrementally. Gives MPO fleets epochs to consolidate pledge or retire pools before the floor binds at 20 % |
| Constitutional fit | Adding a rewards-calc rule is structurally heavier than CIP-0050 or CIP-0023. HFC-style action under Conway-era rules |

## 5. Interaction audit

### 5.1 Same-layer — CIP-0037 ↔ CIP-0050

Technical composition:

$$\sigma'^{(50+37)}_{i} = \min\left(\sigma_{i},\ \sigma^{\text{dyn,sat}}(p_i),\ L\cdot p_{i}\right)$$

**Not canonical without explicit governance adoption.** Alternative formulations of the same §3.2/§3.4 intent:

| Style | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| CIP-0037 | Smooth function, three anchor parameters, soft floor at 20 % of K |
| CIP-0050 | Binary inequality, single parameter, hard break at zero pledge |

**Design-choice asymmetry.**

- CIP-0037 is more forgiving of zero-pledge pools (they keep 20 % of K); CIP-0050 is stricter (they get zero).
- V2 §3.2 KPIs (pledge-as-signal restoration) better served by CIP-0050's sharper economic signal.
- V2 §3.1 tail-protection better served by CIP-0037's floor.

A V2 package that wants both properties would layer the two, at the cost of joint-calibration complexity. A head-to-head comparison is maintained as a separate working document.

### 5.2 Cross-layer — CIP-0037 ↔ CIP-0023 / CIP-0082

Composes cleanly with either fee-layer candidate. Layers sequentially ordered: stake-cap produces $\sigma'^{(37)}$ → reward curve produces $PoolPot^{\text{actual}}_i$ → fee-layer split applies. No precedence rule required.

**End-to-end dependency-chain pairing:** **CIP-0082 (fee layer) + CIP-0037 (stake-cap layer)** — alternative to the CIP-0082 + CIP-0050 pairing. The stake-cap choice is a sharpness-vs-smoothness trade-off, not an incompatibility.

### 5.3 Interaction with `k`

`orig_sat = total_supply / k` is the reference scale inside the formula. A `k` increase:

- Shrinks `orig_sat`.
- Compresses the absolute-ADA range over which the slope region operates.
- Tightens the 500 k-ADA anchor's implied pledge-ratio requirement.

**Simultaneous `k` and CIP-0037 activation requires joint recalibration of $(e, \ell, p_{100\%})$, not independent updates.**

### 5.4 Dependency-chain ordering

1. Fee-layer instrument (CIP-0082 staged path) — secures §3.1 + §3.3.
2. CIP-0037, ramped from $e = 1.0$ toward $e \in [0.2, 0.25]$ — activates §3.2 + §3.4 pool-level.
3. `k` calibration (CIP-0082 stages 3–4) — after steps 1–2, accepting joint $(e, \ell, p_{100\%})$ recalibration at step 3.

## 6. Gaps against V2

| Gap | Why |
| --- | --- |
| §3.1 guarantee | The 20 %-of-K floor softens impact on small pools but does not address fixed-fee regressivity; pairing with CIP-0082 required |
| Custodial constraint | Large-pledge custodial operators (CEX/IVaaS) unconstrained by the curve — §3.4 entity-level gap |
| §4.1, §4.2 | No effect on pot composition or fee-generating population |
| Parameter-surface complexity | Three anchor parameters vs CIP-0050's single $L$ — steady-state calibration harder across §4.3 price scenarios |

**Role in a V2 package.** The **smooth §3.2 instrument** in the bundle: stronger pledge-signal restoration than CIP-0050 in the mid-pledge range, weaker hard-floor protection against zero-pledge Sybil strategies, richer governance surface.
