## Identity

Everstake Validation Services LLC, incorporated in Grand Cayman, founded 2018. Operates as a multi-chain validator and yield infrastructure provider supporting 130+ blockchain networks. Markets Validator-as-a-Service (VaaS) and wallet/yield SDKs to custodians, wallets, and exchanges. Claims 1.6M+ delegators across all chains.

## Profile

Everstake is the largest retail-facing operator in the delegation market by delegator count. 258K delegation addresses averaging 1,730 ADA each — this is individual retail delegators choosing a pool, not managed institutional capital. The contrast with Coinbase (403 delegators, 6.1M ADA/deleg) or Blockdaemon (47 delegators, 6.1M/deleg) is the sharpest in the cell.

The entity prices at 4.0% margin — above the cell median of 2.0% — but retains massive delegation volume. This suggests delegators reach Everstake through wallet integrations and product UX rather than through fee-based comparison. The 1.6M delegator figure across 130+ chains positions them as one of the largest staking intermediaries globally, with Cardano being one chain among many.

## Historical trajectory

Remarkably stable: 1.41% of supply at epoch 400, 1.47% at epoch 618. The flattest trajectory among IVaaS entities across 200+ epochs.

## Pledge

0.57B ADA delegated / ~11M pledged = **51,000:1**. Near-zero self-pledge relative to managed stake — structural for IVaaS scale.
